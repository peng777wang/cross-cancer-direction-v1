How to read this. Direction classes are inferred from MTAG. In the reversed class no cancer reaches genome-wide significance in its own GWAS and the median borrowing is 76-82%, so the reversal is a model prediction rather than established biological antagonism. The borrowed column is the share of a cancer's MTAG estimate that is not present in that cancer's own GWAS; it is shown so a reader can see which numbers are observations and which are predictions.
